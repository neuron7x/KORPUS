JOINT TRAUMA SYSTEM CLINICAL PRACTICE GUIDELINE (JTS CPG) Prolonged Casualty Care Guidelines (CPG ID:91) The Prolonged Casualty Care (PCC) guidelines are a consolidated list of casualty-centric knowledge, skills, and best practices intended to serve as the DoD baseline clinical practice guidance to guide casualty management over a prolonged amount of time in austere, remote, or expeditionary settings, and/or during long-distance movements.
