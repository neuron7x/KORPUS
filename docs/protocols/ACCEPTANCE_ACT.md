## Acceptance criteria

Record every criterion as pass/fail/waived. A waiver has an owner, rationale, expiry
date and compensating control. Verbal acceptance does not override a failed critical
security or evidence-integrity gate.

## Sign-off

Product owner, engineering owner, corpus/domain reviewer, security owner, operator.
