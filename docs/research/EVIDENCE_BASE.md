# Engineering evidence base

Reviewed: 2026-07-31. Institution names generate hypotheses, not authority. Implementation is justified only by explicit mechanisms, reproducible evaluation and operational evidence.

## Evaluation

- Stanford HELM: broad coverage, explicit incompleteness, multi-metric evaluation and standardized adaptation.
- Google DeepMind FACTS: factuality and grounding require more than citation presence.
- RAGChecker: diagnose retrieval and generation separately.
- ALCE: citation correctness and completeness are distinct.
- xAI Grok 4.1 publication: blind production-distribution evaluation and atomic-claim factuality supplement public benchmarks.

## Risk and abstention

- Google DeepMind Frontier Safety Framework: named capability thresholds, early-warning evaluations and explicit mitigation gates.
- uncertainty-aware abstention research: controlled thresholds require finite-sample evidence rather than intuition.
- NIST AI RMF / GenAI Profile: govern, map, measure and manage across the lifecycle.

## Systems

- Harvard DASlab CS265: co-design storage, indexing, retrieval, workload and hardware; earn system complexity from measured workload.
- NIST SSDF and SP 800-218A: code, models, prompts, datasets and indexes are supply-chain components.
- CISA Secure by Design: safe defaults are product requirements, not optional user configuration.
