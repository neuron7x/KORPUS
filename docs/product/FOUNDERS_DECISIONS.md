# Founders decisions required before beta

Record signed decisions for:

1. ownership percentages and vesting;
2. pre-existing and newly created IP;
3. rights and warranties for contributed data;
4. cash contributions, expenses and reimbursement;
5. revenue, salaries and distributions;
6. control of domain, cloud, stores, payment and source-control accounts;
7. security and incident responsibilities;
8. decision rights and deadlock procedure;
9. confidentiality and permitted disclosures;
10. departure, disability, conflict and buyout;
11. governing entity and jurisdiction;
12. product claims each founder may make publicly.

This repository is technical evidence, not legal advice or a substitute for counsel.
