# Discovery record: David conversation and corpus audit

Source: Telegram export dated 2026-07-31, including text, 55 voice messages, one
round-video message, five images and an HTML concept mock-up. This document records
product intent; it is not a legal partnership agreement.

## Observed problem evidence

David reports that service members currently search large documents or ask peers in
topic groups, while general chatbots can return civilian, incomplete or unsupported
answers. He also reports repeated administrative documents in his unit and identifies
instructor lesson planning as a distinct workflow.

## Proposed value

- immediate question-to-source interface;
- explicit refusal where the corpus lacks evidence;
- administrative draft assistance;
- instructor plans, learning paths and assessments;
- weak-network usability;
- distribution through approximately 14 existing groups, estimated by David at
  5–7 thousand distinct reachable people.

Reach estimates and willingness to pay are hypotheses, not validated market data.

## Working role split inferred from the conversation

- Technical/product partner: architecture, implementation, data research, agents,
  infrastructure, tests, documentation and model/provider operations.
- Domain/distribution partner (David): user problems, specialist validation within
  competence, corpus leads, field testing, community distribution and proposed seed
  funding.

The conversation contains agreement to start ("тоді так і працюємо") but does not
define equity, revenue split, IP ownership, account control, liabilities or exit. Those
must be settled in a separate written founders agreement before money or IP becomes
material.

## Product corrections to early claims

- An LLM constrained by RAG can still misretrieve or misstate; it is not guaranteed to
  avoid invention.
- USD 240 is a tooling hypothesis, not total cost of a secure production service.
- A 2–4 week period can produce a pilot, not a validated cross-domain system over the
  whole corpus.
- Buying or receiving files is not itself evidence of redistribution or commercial
  transformation rights.
- Passing an AI-generated quiz is not military certification.
