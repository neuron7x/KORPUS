# Act 01 — works executed

The package contains the following implemented work, not merely a roadmap:

1. FastAPI trust kernel and dependency injection.
2. Server-derived OIDC identity and content-addressed entitlement projection.
3. ABAC, compartments and PostgreSQL RLS contracts before retrieval.
4. Immutable document/version/span data model with temporal supersession.
5. Streaming quarantine ingestion, MIME/signature checks, malware-scanner contract, parser subprocess and bounded OCR.
6. Durable ingestion jobs with lease, retry and dead-letter states.
7. Source authenticity, near-duplicate and extraction-quality governance.
8. Scoped, expiring and revocable reviewer credentials with separation of duties.
9. Corpus policy controlling classification, allowed operations, retention, legal hold and embedding egress.
10. Bounded lexical retrieval, optional calibrated semantic fusion, MMR and deterministic ordering.
11. Exact claim-to-span citations, contradiction checks and evidence-bound abstention.
12. Hash-chained audit, CAS head, transactional outbox and remote-anchor contract.
13. Docker Compose development topology and Kubernetes/Kustomize production reference topology.
14. GitLab CI contracts, backup/restore tools, SBOM/scanner hooks and release packaging.
15. Complete v4 audit plus machine classification of all 99 findings in v5.

Evidence and limitations are recorded in `reports/`, `VERIFICATION_REPORT_V5.md` and `docs/audit/closure/`.
